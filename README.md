## About

I am an **incoming PhD student (Fall 2027)** at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), HKUST(GZ).

I am a member of the [Data Intelligence and Analytics Lab (DIAL)](https://github.com/HKUSTDial), led by [Prof. Yuyu Luo](https://luoyuyu.vip/). The lab develops data intelligence systems at the intersection of data and AI.

My research interests include **Multimodal Coding**, **CUA Agent**, and **Game Agent**.

You can find my publications and projects on my [personal website](https://sxy1499894281.github.io/#multimodal-coding).
